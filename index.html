How a lead becomes a client

The same six steps for every lead, whether you or a partner works it.

1 · Mon

Find

Use the sources in "Find leads". Add businesses opened in the last 12 months.

2

Score

Fit + need + timing, 0–2 each. 5–6 is sample-first, 4 is mass, 3 or less waits.

3

Claim

A partner claims it and gets 14 days of exclusivity. Log every touch.

4

Reach out

Use the channel on the lead, and the message the app writes for it.

5 · 48 h

Sample or offer

Sample-first: call, then a 2-hour sample. Mass: the fixed-price offer.

6

Close

Present the sample live, send the 50% deposit link the same day.
